How evolution works

Each target lists its conditions. Key Points = how many of the listed conditions you must meet (stats, weight, mistakes, bond, discipline, wins, key Digimon). Plain numbers are minimums; ≤ means “at most”. Weight is in grams.

Key Digimon means your partner must currently be (have evolved from) that Digimon.

Rough timing: Rookie ≈12 h after hatching · Champion ≈3–4 days later · Ultimate ≈3 days · Mega ≈4 days (only if conditions are met — otherwise it stays).

Use GrapLeomon's Digivolution Dojo in Floatia to see how close you are (Lv.2 can block paths), or Antylamon to reveal conditions. Digivolution Cores force a form but give no lifespan bonus.

Mistake forms: Rookie with STR > WIS that meets nothing → Numemon; WIS > STR → Geremon; full poop gauge → Sukamon (Rookie) / PlatinumSukamon (Champion).

How recruiting works + early priorities

Recruitable Digimon show as a blue “!” on the map. Most want a fight, an item, or a small quest. Each one raises Floatia's prosperity and usually opens a service or shop.

Get these first (Chapter 1):

  • Tentomon (Item Shop) – just buy a 100-bit disc.
  • Kabuterimon (better Training Hall) and Togemon / Kuwagamon (free training veggies daily).
  • Veemon (building upgrades) → needs Togemon's quest first.
  • Wormmon (Fishing Rod) → then Seadramon and Renamon.
  • Greymon (Tent: rest & cook anywhere) and Birdramon (fast travel).
  • Numemon (free toilets) and Patamon (warehouse).

Chapter 2: GrapLeomon opens the Digivolution Dojo + Ohguino Wastelands; Paildramon unlocks DNA digivolution; Guardromon crafts recovery items; Lab upgrades unlock skill teachers (AeroVeedramon, MetalMamemon, MetalSeadramon, Ophanimon).

Postgame: Royal Knights & Demon Lords join the Dimensional Dungeon and hand out chips, cores, bits and lifespan.

Shop unlocks
Item Shop: Tentomon (recovery), Gargomon (plugins/boosters), Cherubimon · BanchoLeomon · Darkdramon (cores). Upgrading the shop (Veemon) adds stock tiers.
Hospital: Angemon opens it; Kyubimon sells care items; Advanced Item Shop: LadyDevimon (Devil Chips), HerculesKabuterimon (Mini Chips), WereGarurumon (cores), Sakuyamon (drinks).
Colosseum: Leomon sells recovery + plugins. Arena: Rapidmon swaps Luck Coins for prizes.
